% texforge example — a formal letter using the standard `letter` class.
% Renders with: texforge render examples/letter/main.tex
\documentclass[11pt,a4paper]{letter}

\usepackage[margin=2.5cm]{geometry}
\usepackage{microtype}
\usepackage{xcolor}

\signature{Ada Lovelace}
\address{Ada Lovelace\\12 Analytical Way\\London, UK}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\begin{letter}{Charles Babbage\\Dorset Street\\London, UK}

\opening{Dear Mr.\ Babbage,}

Thank you for the memoir on the Analytical Engine. I have appended a set of
notes considerably longer than the original text, in which I set out a method
for computing the Bernoulli numbers by machine.

I believe the engine's significance is widely under-appreciated: it does not
merely calculate, but manipulates symbols according to rules, and might in
principle act upon anything whose relations can be expressed. This letter was
itself typeset by a self-healing engine that fetched every package it needed on
demand --- a small echo of the idea.

I look forward to your thoughts.

\closing{With warm regards,}

\ps{P.S. The full working is enclosed in the accompanying report.}

\end{letter}
\end{document}
